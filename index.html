Common Questions

How fast is delivery?

Same day inside Cairo for orders before 2 pm, and 2–3 days elsewhere in Egypt.

Can I return a book?

Yes, within 7 days if the book is unread and in its original condition.

Do you buy used books?

We do. Bring them in and get store credit after a quick check.
